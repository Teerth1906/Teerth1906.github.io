JARVIS

JARVIS is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own Mac.

Privacy policy

JARVIS reads the owner's Gmail with read-only access, only to answer the owner's own questions. It never sends, deletes or changes email, and it shares nothing with anyone else.

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's Mac and can be removed at any time from the owner's Google Account settings (Security, Third-party access).
